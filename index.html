How the plan flows

Three required workouts rotate freely; phases advance as you complete them.

Flexible rotation

Instead of fixed weekdays, train in this order whenever it suits your week:

1. Lower A2. Upper3. Lower BOptional: anytime after Lower B

Completing all three advances your training phase automatically. Skipping a day simply continues from the same point later.

Four-phase cycle

Phase 1 AdaptationPhase 2 ProgressionPhase 3 ProductivePhase 4 Recovery

Three progressive phases are followed by one easier phase. A period start also triggers an easy recommendation immediately, regardless of phase.

Duration modes

  • Express (40–50 min): essential exercises only
  • Standard (60–75 min): essential + recommended
  • Complete (75–90 min): full session including optional work

Exercise swaps

Every exercise has approved alternatives — pick whichever equipment is free or feels best. Bulgarian split squats, walking/reverse lunges and free-weight squats are intentionally excluded.

Ranges: 1,850–2,050 kcal · Protein 95–110g · Fat 55–65g · Carbohydrates 220–270g. Adjust only after two consecutive weeks of trend data, not a single day.

Portion-based structure

  • Protein: one palm-sized portion per main meal
  • Carbohydrates: one to two cupped-hand portions per main meal
  • Vegetables or fruit: one to two fist-sized portions
  • Fat: roughly one thumb-sized portion
  • Add one protein + carbohydrate snack around training

Execution guide

Simple rules that keep the plan safe and effective.

RIR made simple

  • 4 RIR: ~4 clean reps remained
  • 2 RIR: ~2 remained
  • 1 RIR: ~1 remained
  • 0 RIR: failure — avoided by default

Progression

Keep the same load until every set reaches the top of its range at the assigned RIR, then increase by the smallest available increment.

Cramps or low energy

Use the Easy recommendation, reduce load ~10–15%, or switch to Express mode. Walking and light cycling remain excellent low-impact options.

When to seek care

Severe or worsening pain, dizziness, unusually heavy bleeding, or symptoms that repeatedly prevent normal activity should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

Safety note

This is general fitness guidance, not medical treatment or a menstrual-health diagnostic tool. Stop training and seek qualified medical assessment for chest pain, dizziness, unusual shortness of breath, sharp pain, very heavy bleeding, or symptoms that persist or worsen.
